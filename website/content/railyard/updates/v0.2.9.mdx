<ChangelogSection type="features">

- Added support for showing driving paths on modded maps (**[gcm](https://github.com/martig7)**)
- Improved handling of delayed Steam launches
  - Railyard now watches for the Steam game process to launch after starting and timeouts after a minute of no gaming being seen to avoid hanging state
  - An in progress launch can now be cancelled

</ChangelogSection>

<ChangelogSection type="improvements">

- Expanded and hardened frontend logging
- Overhauled map data quality reporting to be more granular and no longer rely on self-reports
- Removed `Level of Detail` tags
- Asset conflicts are now per asset, meaning an approved replacement no longer gets superseded/dropped by a concurrent update

</ChangelogSection>

<ChangelogSection type="bugfixes">

- Fixed a bug where navigating away from the versions info of an asset would cause Railyard to black screen
- Fixed a bug where the in-progress download toast would hang indefinitely
- Fixed Steam not working on Linux due to missing paths (**[Mari](https://github.com/mellowagain)**)

</ChangelogSection>
