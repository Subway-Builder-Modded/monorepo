The Registry maintains a **quality floor**: once maps of a certain data quality exist for a country, new submissions for that country are expected to meet or exceed it. The goal is to make sure creators use the best data available/discovered for a country, and that newer maps are always backed by better data than their predecessors.

## The Policy {#policy}

1. **Country floor.** A new map for a country must reach a data-quality tier **at or above the country's established floor**. This floor is the tier the country's existing maps demonstrate is achievable with country-wide data. If a `high-quality` pipeline covers a country, a new `low-quality` map of another city in that country will not be accepted.
2. **City-specific exception.** Where a city's higher tier comes from **city-specific data** that is not available countrywide, that city's tier floors only that city and not the whole country.
3. **Same-city rule (absolute).** A new map of a city is never accepted below the best existing tier for that same city, regardless of how that tier was achieved.
4. **Unscored maps set no floor.** Legacy maps still showing `unknown-quality` do not establish a floor; only reviewed tiers count.

:::example[Istanbul and Ankara]
Suppose Istanbul has a `medium-quality` map built on city-specific data (say, a municipal transport survey that exists only for Istanbul), and no country-wide Turkish pipeline of that quality exists.

- A new **Ankara** map at `low-quality` is **allowed** — Istanbul's tier came from city-specific data, so it does not set the floor for the rest of Turkey.
- A new **Istanbul** map at `low-quality` is **not allowed** — Istanbul itself has demonstrated `medium-quality` is achievable, so new Istanbul maps must meet it.
  :::

## How the Floor Is Applied {#floor-application}

The floor is evaluated during **data-quality review**. This is the step every new submission goes through before it can be published (see [Data Quality](/registry/docs/data-quality/overview#how-tiers-are-assigned)). When a maintainer confirms a new map's tier, they see the country's existing maps and tiers side by side; a submission below the applicable floor is flagged and will not be accepted unless an exception applies.

Whether a higher-tier map's data is "city-specific" is up to the maintainers' judgment, and will be made from the scored answers and methodology on record for the existing maps. If you believe an exception applies to your submission, please specify why in **Methodology** field, as that is the place maintainers read for details that clarify ambiguous cases.

:::tip[Raising the floor]
The floor is about the country's demonstrated available quality of dataa sources, not about gatekeeping authors. If your pipeline falls below the floor, the [Data Quality Questions guide](/registry/docs/data-quality/submission-questions) and the [scoring rubric](/registry/docs/data-quality/scoring-rubric) show exactly which factors may be holding your pipeline's quality back the most. Finding a better population table, or forcing estimated flows to add up to real totals, will help move up the data quality of a map.
:::
